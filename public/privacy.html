Privacy policy

How Imara Capital handles the details you share through this website.

What we collect

When you use the eligibility check, nothing leaves your browser. When you request a call back, we collect your name, phone number, business name, preferred language, and the loan amount, term and eligibility answers you selected.

How we use it

Only to contact you about your application, assess it, and give you a written offer. We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Your account

If you create an account, we store your email address, a securely hashed password (we never see the password itself), your profile (contact details, business details and, if you add one, a profile photo) and the applications you send while logged in. Your photo is stored privately and is never publicly viewable. Only you and authorised Imara staff can see them. Log-in sessions are kept in your browser so you stay logged in; logging out removes them from that device, and "Log out on all devices" ends every session.

Where it is stored

Website form submissions are processed and stored on our behalf by our hosting provider, Netlify. Account and application data is stored with our database provider, Supabase.

Your rights

Under Kenya's Data Protection Act, 2019, you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its processing, at any time. Speak to your Imara officer or any of our branches.

Licensing & complaints

If you are unhappy with any part of our service, tell your officer or visit a branch in Nairobi, Mombasa, Nakuru or Eldoret. We acknowledge every complaint within two business days.
