08 · Straight answers

What you'd ask across the table.

Do I need collateral?+

No. We lend against your trading record — your M-Pesa and bank statements — not your title deed, your logbook or a guarantor's property.

What documents do I need?+

Six months of M-Pesa statements, six months of bank statements, your business registration certificate and your national ID. No audited accounts. No stamped business plan.

How is my repayment calculated?+

Interest is charged monthly on the balance you still owe, so it falls as you repay. The indicative rate is 2% a month (about 24% a year) — above bank rates of 12–17%, because there is no collateral and no months of waiting. Your offer lists the monthly installment, the total repayment, the one-time processing fee and the excise duty on it. Nothing is added later.

How fast does the money arrive?+

10 to 60 days from a complete application. Loans under KSh 1 million usually land in 10–14 days. Larger amounts take longer because we visit the business first.

What if a month is slow?+

Call your officer before the due date. Mwisho wa mwezi is hard for every trader — we would rather agree a new date with you than surprise you with a penalty.

Can I speak Kiswahili with my officer?+

Ndiyo. Every Imara officer works in Kiswahili and English, and your offer can be printed in either.
